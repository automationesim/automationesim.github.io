Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

FLI Automation is a private workflow automation tool. It is operated by a single individual for internal business record keeping and is not offered to the public.

What the application accesses

With the explicit consent of the account owner, the application accesses the following Google services on that owner's own account:

  • Google Drive: to read purchase order files uploaded to a designated folder, and to rename and move those files.
  • Google Sheets: to record purchase order, delivery and payment details in spreadsheets owned by the account holder.
  • Gmail: to send and label internal notification emails to the account holder.
  • Google Calendar: to read delivery events for scheduling records.

How the information is used

Information is used only to perform the automation described above. It is processed within the account owner's own Google account and a private n8n workflow instance controlled by the account owner.

Sharing

No information obtained from Google APIs is sold, rented or transferred to any third party. No information is used for advertising. No person other than the account owner reads the data.

Limited Use

FLI Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained in the account owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets for as long as the owner chooses to keep them. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends all access by this application.
